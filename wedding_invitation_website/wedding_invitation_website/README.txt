WEDDING INVITATION WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN:
1. Download and unzip wedding_invitation_website.zip.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.
4. Internet is needed for the Google Fonts and sample wedding photos. The page itself runs locally.

HOW TO CUSTOMIZE:
- Open index.html in Notepad or VS Code.
- Replace "Alexandra" and "Ryan" with your names.
- Update the date, ceremony time, venue and RSVP deadline.
- In style.css, replace the image URLs (search for images.unsplash.com) with your own image URLs.
- The RSVP form is a front-end demo only. It does not email or store submissions. To receive replies, connect it to a form service or a backend.

FILES:
index.html  - website structure and invitation text
style.css   - layout, colours, typography and responsive design
script.js   - mobile menu and demo RSVP interaction
