Sustainable Solutions & Mitigation

Rainwater Harvesting

Recharge aquifers by capturing runoff & rooftop rain. Gujarat's "Sujalam Sufalam" scheme success.

Drip Irrigation

Reduce farm water use by 30–60%, minimize extraction pressure. Adopted in Saurashtra.

Limit Over-pumping

Regulatory frameworks & extraction permits based on aquifer sustainability. CGWB monitoring.

Water Reuse

Treated wastewater for irrigation & industrial cooling reduces freshwater demand.

Afforestation

Enhances infiltration, reduces runoff, improves soil moisture retention.

IoT Smart Monitoring

Real-time sensors & AI for extraction control & early warnings (pilot in Gujarat).

Impact of solutions: Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR) can recover up to 15% of depleted volumes annually.
